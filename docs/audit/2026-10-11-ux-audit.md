# UX audit: ease of use and speed

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Scope: staff app (`apps/web`), customer QR menu (`apps/qr`). This was a read-only
review. Evidence comes from the 59 manual screenshots (`docs/user-manual/images/`) and the code.
Screenshot names are given without the folder. Code paths are relative to `apps/web/src` unless they
start with `apps/qr`.

## 1. Executive summary

- **The counter's main path is already fast and clear.** The money handling is careful.
  - A dine-in order takes about 4 taps plus one tap per dish: table, guest count, dishes, *Send to
    kitchen*.
  - Settling is 3 taps: *Bill*, *Cash*, *Confirm ₹X cash · change ₹Y*. It has C/U/K/Enter/Esc keys,
    live change, and the confirm button names the amount and mode (`features/billing/components/SettlePanel.tsx:129-134`).
  - Voids, discounts and dish cancels all need a reason (`26-void.png`, `25-discount.png`,
    `28-cancel-dish.png`).
  - The labels are in plain English, and ₹, GST and UPI are used correctly throughout.
- **The waiter's phone flow is the weakest core task.** On the Captain screen, the order panel sits
  above the menu and nothing sticks to the bottom of the screen (`features/service/components/OrderWorkspace.tsx:65-76`,
  `46-captain-order.png`). After adding dishes, the waiter has to scroll back up to find *Send to
  kitchen*, and the running total cannot be seen while picking dishes.
- **Nothing in the product makes a sound.** No code calls `Audio` or `vibrate` anywhere. A new KOT,
  a new QR order and "food ready" only show a toast or change a count (`features/orders/components/qr/QrInbox.tsx:21-30`,
  `features/orders/useReadyAlerts.ts`). A busy kitchen or counter will miss them. QR orders also wait
  for staff approval, so a missed order leaves a guest waiting.
- **The product is English-only and has no plan for other languages.** No i18n library is set up and
  every string is hard-coded. Many kitchen staff and waiters in Indian restaurants read Hindi or a
  regional language first, and the QR menu has no language switch either. This is the largest
  barrier to selling the product.
- **Learning in the first week is good, but some places are dead ends or unsafe.**
  - The Setup guide is good (`79-setup-guide.png`).
  - Dead ends:
    - *Merge table* opens even when no other table is running (`15-merge-table.png`).
    - A bad QR link shows "Some fields are not valid." (`94-qr-bad-link.png`).
    - Empty ₹0 orders block day close (`30-day-close.png`).
  - Unsafe spots:
    - Tiny text links for *Cancel* and *Reprint* on sent dishes (`order-panel/KotCard.tsx:116-123`).
    - No undo on a kitchen bump (`features/kitchen/pages/KdsPage.tsx:26-27`).
    - Marking a dish unavailable needs a separate page.

## 2. Findings

Severity:
- **High** blocks or slows a core task every service, or risks a lost order or money.
- **Medium** costs time or causes confusion.
- **Low** is polish.

## 3. Quick wins (each ≤ 1 day)

1. UX-2 and UX-3: a chime for a new KOT, a new QR order and food ready. Add phone vibrate for
   waiters and a sound toggle.
2. UX-8: turn *Cancel* and *Reprint* into 44 px buttons, and add a red banner when a KOT fails to
   print.
3. UX-5: larger KDS dish and note text, plus a "Large text" toggle.
4. UX-19: preset reason chips for void, cancel and discount.
5. UX-12 and UX-11: no reason needed to cancel an order with no dishes, an optional guest count, and
   "Clear empty orders" on Day close.
6. UX-17 and UX-18: reorder the bill buttons with Void on the right, and move *Cancel order* away
   from *Send to kitchen*.
7. UX-22: guest-friendly QR error text, with no *Try again* on 4xx errors.
8. UX-23: disable *Merge table* when there is nothing to merge.
9. UX-25: correct offline wording, plus a full-width offline banner.
10. UX-24: a visible *Switch staff* chip and an idle auto-lock on counters.
11. UX-21: a denomination counter for day close.
12. UX-28 and UX-29: whole rupees on report tiles, and a scroll hint on the category chips.

## 4. Bigger bets

- **B1. Sticky "send bar" for Captain on phones (UX-1), M.** This is the waiter's main tool, used
  dozens of times per shift. A bottom bar showing the new-dish count, total and *Send* keeps the
  thumb in reach and gives a running view of the order. This one change probably saves the most
  time in the product.
- **B2. Language support (UX-4), L.** This is needed to sell beyond English-speaking owners.
  - Do it per device: kitchen and waiter screens in Hindi or a regional language, the back office in
    English.
  - Add a "local name" field for dishes on KOT and QR.
  - Add a language switch on the QR menu.
  - Start now: about 600 strings today, and the number grows with every feature.
- **B3. A sound and attention system (UX-2, UX-3), S–M.** One `useAlertSound(event, sound)` hook with
  separate sounds for new KOT, cancel, QR order and ready. Add a "tap to enable sound" unlock,
  per-device volume and mute, and repeat every 30 s until someone acts on a QR order. Restaurants
  judge a POS partly by whether it "rings".
- **B4. Working through short outages (UX-25), L.** The server is on the local network, so outages are
  short (a router or PC restart).
  - Keep orders that have not been sent yet in IndexedDB, and send them when the server is back.
  - Keep the last menu and floor plan readable.
  - Allow "Print KOT locally" for counter-only sending.
  - Payments should stay online-only.
  - At minimum, never lose dishes the cashier has already added.
- **B5. Faster dish entry for experts (UX-9, UX-10, UX-30), M.** Short codes, size buttons on the
  tile, keyboard shortcuts and "repeat last round" for a table. The aim is for a trained cashier to
  enter a 6-dish order in under 15 seconds without touching the screen. Competing Indian POS
  products sell on this.
- **B6. Sold-out from the order screen (UX-7), M.** Long-press a tile to mark it sold out. Sold-out
  tiles stay visible, greyed out. The QR menu shows "Sold out" instead of hiding the dish. This
  removes a separate trip to the Availability page during a rush.
- **B7. Learning on the job, M.**
  - Add a "Practice mode" toggle per device. It writes to a training outlet so new staff can take
    fake orders without touching real reports.
  - Add a role-specific 5-step walkthrough the first time someone signs in with a PIN ("This is your
    table list. Tap a table…").
  - The current Setup guide covers the owner well, but has nothing for the staff who join in
    week 1.
- **B8. UPI that is reliable (UX-14), M.** A dynamic UPI QR with the amount filled in, on screen and
  on the bill. Later, a payment-provider integration that confirms the payment automatically.
